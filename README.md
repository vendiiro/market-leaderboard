# market-leaderboard

A planned local dashboard that ranks hand-picked watchlists like a leaderboard and shows how a mix of them spreads
risk across sectors, countries and currencies:

- US big tech
- European defence
- European energy
- Nordic energy
- Finnish smaller companies (about 0.3-5 billion EUR market cap)

Educational research tool: it shows screen results and exposure views, never investment advice.

Status: design and plan only (`docs/spec.md`, `docs/plan.md`). See `START_HERE.md` to begin building.
Data code is adapted from [dip-dashboard](https://github.com/vendiiro/dip-dashboard).
